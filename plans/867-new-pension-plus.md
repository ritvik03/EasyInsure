**One-line summary:** A market-linked pension plan that accumulates a fund through single or regular premiums and converts it into an annuity at vesting, with no life sum assured and restricted access before retirement.

## Premiums, purchase price and annuity

- **Premium options:** single premium, or regular premium by yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly NACH.
- **Minimum regular premium:** ₹30,000 yearly, ₹16,000 half-yearly, ₹9,000 quarterly, ₹3,000 monthly.
- **Minimum single premium:** ₹1,00,000.
- **Multiples:** regular premiums in ₹1,000 multiples (₹250 for monthly); single premium in ₹10,000 multiples.
- **Maximum premium:** no limit, subject to financial underwriting.
- **Sum assured:** not applicable; this is a pension accumulation plan with no Basic Sum Assured.

There is no published premium rate table. The policyholder chooses the premium, term and vesting age, and the fund value at vesting is used to buy an annuity. The brochure publishes two sample benefit illustrations, one regular premium and one single premium, each at 4% and 8% p.a.

## Benefits

### Vesting benefit

On survival to the vesting date, the Unit Fund Value vests. It cannot be taken as a lump sum in normal circumstances: it must be used to buy an immediate or deferred annuity from LIC, or up to 60% may be commuted and the balance annuitised. Up to 50% of the proceeds net of commutation may be used to buy an annuity from another insurer. If the proceeds are insufficient to purchase the minimum annuity permitted under IRDAI rules, they may be paid as a lump sum. If the policy is in reduced paid-up status at vesting, the Unit Fund Value is still annuitised.

### Death benefit

On death before vesting while the policy is in force, the higher of:

1. Unit Fund Value on the date of intimation of death; or
2. 105% of total premiums received to date, less partial withdrawals made in the two years before death.

The nominee may withdraw the entire proceeds, use them to buy an immediate or deferred annuity, or take the death benefit in instalments over up to five years. If the policy was discontinued after the lock-in, the death benefit is the higher of Unit Fund Value or the Paid-up Sum Assured (105% of premiums to the first unpaid premium less recent partial withdrawals).

### Annuity and pension options at vesting

- **Full annuitisation:** use the entire proceeds to buy an immediate or deferred annuity at LIC's then-prevailing rates.
- **Commutation:** commute up to 60% and use the balance to buy an immediate or deferred annuity.
- **Open-market option:** buy an annuity from another insurer for up to 50% of the proceeds net of commutation, after informing LIC before vesting.
- **Deferred annuity:** available as an alternative to an immediate annuity.
- **Extension of vesting:** may be exercised if the life assured is below 60 when opting, the total of original and extended terms is at most 42 years, and the request is made at least three months before vesting. Vesting can be extended with or without further premiums; without further premiums guaranteed additions are reduced pro rata.
- **Minimum annuity:** if the fund cannot buy the minimum annuity, the proceeds may be paid as a lump sum.
- **Annuity rate:** not guaranteed at entry; it depends on the plan and rates in force at vesting.

## Surrender, loan, paid-up and revival

- **Surrender:** allowed at any time, but proceeds are not paid in cash. Within five years the Unit Fund Value less discontinuance charge goes to the Pension Discontinued Policy Fund; its proceeds at the end of the lock-in are used to buy an annuity. After five years the Unit Fund Value is used to buy an annuity. If the life assured dies after surrender but before the lock-in ends, the fund proceeds are paid to the nominee immediately.
- **Loan:** not available.
- **Paid-up:** if regular premiums stop after five years, the policy becomes reduced paid-up after the grace period. The Paid-up Sum Assured for death benefit is 105% of premiums paid to the first unpaid premium less partial withdrawals in the two years before death; guaranteed additions are not paid in reduced paid-up status. The fund remains invested for annuitisation at vesting.
- **Revival:** within three years of the first unpaid premium and before vesting, subject to underwriting and payment of due premiums.
- **Free look:** 30 days.
- **Grace period:** 30 days for yearly, half-yearly and quarterly; 15 days for monthly NACH.
- **Compulsory termination:** if the policy has run five years with five full years' premiums paid and the fund falls below one annualized premium (regular) or 30% of the single premium, it is compulsorily terminated and the balance is applied to buy an annuity.

## Exclusions and conditions

The stated exclusion is suicide within 12 months of commencement or revival; the nominee receives the Unit Fund Value, subject to the death-benefit utilisation options. There is no waiting or lien period.

The policy is subject to the standard conditions on misstatement, fraud and revival in the policy document. Nomination and assignment are allowed under Sections 39 and 38 of the Insurance Act, 1938. Pension proceeds must be annuitised except for permitted commutation, and partial withdrawals are restricted to specified purposes.

## Tax treatment

The CIS and brochure do not state Section 80C, 80CCC or Section 10(10D) eligibility. They say statutory taxes on charges apply at the prevailing rate and that income-tax benefits and implications should be checked with a tax adviser. The benefit illustrations state that the prevailing tax charge (GST) is currently exempted. Annuity income is taxable under the prevailing law, which the documents do not describe. Confirm the current treatment before relying on it.

## Suitability and decision notes

**Best suited to:** long-horizon retirement savers who want a market-linked accumulation phase followed by an annuity, and who may want to commute part of the fund at vesting.

**Poor fit for:** anyone needing liquidity or cash access (surrender proceeds must be annuitised), buyers seeking guaranteed pension or guaranteed returns, people who need life cover (there is no Basic Sum Assured), and short-term investors.

**Key trade-offs:** the five-year lock-in with compulsory annuitisation of proceeds; full market risk with no guaranteed vesting amount; no life sum assured; partial withdrawals limited to specified purposes and three times in total; and annuity rates at vesting are not locked in at entry. The plan also has no riders and does not allow top-ups.

Core formulas:

```text
Death Benefit          = max(unit_fund_value,
                             1.05 × total_premiums_paid
                               − partial_withdrawals_last_2y)
Vesting Benefit        = unit_fund_value_at_vesting
Commutation            = min(0.60 × vesting_benefit, amount_chosen)
Annuity Purchase Price = vesting_benefit − commutation
GA (regular)           = annualized_premium × ga_rate(year)
GA (single)            = single_premium × ga_rate(year)
Paid-up Sum Assured    = 1.05 × premiums_paid_to_fup
                         − partial_withdrawals_last_2y
Surrender (after 5y)   = unit_fund_value, used to buy an annuity
Surrender (before 5y)  = (unit_fund_value − discontinuance_charge)
                         accumulated in the Pension Discontinued Policy Fund
                         until the end of the 5-year lock-in, then annuitised
```
