# LIC's New Jeevan Dhara II — Plan 892

**One-line summary:** A non-par deferred annuity that accepts regular or single premiums, lets the buyer choose a 1-15 year deferment period and one of 11 annuity options, and then pays a guaranteed lifetime annuity with optional return of premium.

## Purchase price and annuity rates

- **Premium types:** regular premium (options 1-9) or single premium (options 10-11).
- **Deferment period:** 2-15 years for regular premium, 1-15 years for single premium. Nothing is paid during deferment; the annuity starts at vesting.
- **Annuity modes:** yearly, half-yearly, quarterly and monthly, payable in arrears. Modal factors are 1.00, 0.98, 0.97 and 0.96.
- **Minimum annuity:** ₹1,000 monthly, ₹3,000 quarterly, ₹6,000 half-yearly or ₹12,000 yearly.
- **Minimum premium:** ₹11,000 yearly at the lowest, but it rises by option, deferment period and age band. The minimum instalment premium for other modes is the minimum yearly premium times the premium conversion factor, with a ₹1,000 floor.
- **Minimum purchase price:** ₹1,01,000 at the lowest for standard cases; ₹50,000 for the Divyangjan option.
- **Maximum premium:** no limit, subject to underwriting.
- **Annuity rates:** guaranteed at inception but not published as a full age-wise table. The brochure publishes minimum-premium tables and two sample illustrations only; rates are quoted by LIC's calculator/underwriting.

## Benefits

### Annuity options

| Option | Annuity type | Payout and death benefit |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Single life | Life annuity with no return of premium. Death during deferment: 105% of premiums; after deferment: nil. |
| 2 | Single life | Life annuity with return of premium. Death after deferment: 100% of total premiums paid. |
| 3 | Single life | Life annuity with 50% return of premium on survival to age 75. Death after deferment: total premiums less early ROP paid. |
| 4 | Single life | Life annuity with 100% return of premium on survival to age 75. Death after deferment: total premiums less early ROP paid. |
| 5 | Single life | Life annuity with 50% return of premium on survival to age 80. Death after deferment: total premiums less early ROP paid. |
| 6 | Single life | Life annuity with 100% return of premium on survival to age 80. Death after deferment: total premiums less early ROP paid. |
| 7 | Single life | Life annuity with 5% return of premium each year from age 76 to 95. Death after deferment: total premiums less early ROP paid. |
| 8 | Joint life | Life annuity for the joint lives, then the survivor; no return of premium. Death after deferment: nil. |
| 9 | Joint life | Life annuity with return of premium. Death of last survivor: 100% of total premiums paid. |
| 10 | Single life, single premium | Life annuity with return of purchase price. Death after deferment: 100% of purchase price. |
| 11 | Joint life, single premium | Joint life annuity with return of purchase price. Death of last survivor: 100% of purchase price. |

Under every option, the annuity is paid in arrears from the vesting date for as long as the annuitant, or the last survivor for joint life, is alive.

### Death benefit

During the deferment period, all options pay 105% of total premiums paid, subject to a floor equal to the surrender value on the date of death. After the deferment period the benefit follows the option: nil under options 1 and 8; 100% of total premiums paid under options 2 and 9; 100% of total premiums paid less early return of premium already paid under options 3-7; and 100% of the purchase price under options 10 and 11. Under joint life options the benefit is payable on the death of the last survivor.

### Maturity and survival benefit

There is no maturity benefit. The survival benefit is the annuity itself, which begins on the vesting date and continues for life. Options 3-7 add the specified early return of premium on survival to the stated age; option 7 pays 5% of total premiums paid each year from age 76 to 95.

### Top-up annuity

A top-up annuity may be added at any time during the deferment period by paying an additional single premium of at least ₹50,000, provided at least one full year of deferment remains. The top-up is priced at the annuity rates prevailing at the time, using the then age of the annuitant(s) and matching the timing of the base annuity. Each top-up is treated as a separate single-premium contract for incentives and the suicide clause; it cannot be surrendered independently, and its benefits are calculated separately and added to the base policy.

## Optional benefits and riders

- **Riders:** none.
- **Top-up annuity:** minimum ₹50,000 per payment, during the deferment period, at prevailing rates.
- **Liquidity option:** available under options 2, 9, 10 and 11 after 5 years from the first annuity payment. It may be used up to 3 times, with total withdrawals capped at 60% of total premiums excluding top-up. The annuity and other benefits are reduced pro rata, and the revised annuity must satisfy the minimum annuity limits.
- **Advanced annuity option:** available under joint life options 9 and 11 after the deferment period, within 6 months of the first death. Up to 5 years of annuity may be advanced, discounted at 6.78% p.a. compounding half-yearly for 2026-27, with the annuity reduced for the advance period.
- **Death benefit payment options:** lump sum, annuitisation of the death benefit, or instalments over 5, 10 or 15 years. Minimum instalments are ₹5,000 monthly, ₹15,000 quarterly, ₹25,000 half-yearly and ₹50,000 yearly. The interest rate for instalments commencing between 1 May 2026 and 30 April 2027 is 5.11% p.a. effective.
- **Divyangjan option:** Option 10 may be purchased on the proposer's own life for the benefit of a dependant person with disability, with a minimum purchase price of ₹50,000 and no minimum annuity limit.

## Surrender, loan, paid-up and revival

### Surrender

Single premium policies may be surrendered at any time. Regular premium policies with a PPT below 5 years may be surrendered during or after the deferment period once at least one full year's premium has been paid. Policies with a PPT of 5 years or more may be surrendered after the first policy year once at least one full year's premium has been paid.

During the deferment period, surrender is allowed under all options. After the deferment period, surrender is allowed only under options 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 11; nothing is payable under options 1 and 8. The surrender value is the higher of Guaranteed Surrender Value and Special Surrender Value.

- **GSV during deferment:** total premiums paid or purchase price multiplied by the GSV factor.
- **GSV after deferment:** 90% of total premiums paid or purchase price less total annuity paid, and for options 3-7 also less early return of premium paid.
- **GSV factors, options 1-9:** 0% in policy year 1; 90% within one year of vesting; 30% in year 2, 35% in year 3 or 50% in years 4-7 for earlier deferment periods; 90% from policy year 8.
- **GSV factors, options 10-11:** 75% in policy years 1-3 and 90% from year 4 during deferment.

### Loan

A loan is available under the options that include return of premium: options 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 11. Under regular premium with a PPT below 5 years, the loan is available after 3 months from issue or free-look expiry, whichever is later, plus one full year's premium. With a PPT of 5 years or more, it is available after the first policy year. Single premium policies 10 and 11 are eligible after 3 months or free-look expiry. Under joint life, the primary annuitant may avail the loan first and the secondary annuitant after the primary's death.

The effective annual interest may not exceed 50% of the annual annuity and the total loan may not exceed 80% of the surrender value including top-up. The loan interest rate for 2026-27 is 9.50% p.a. compounding half-yearly.

### Paid-up, revival, grace and free look

- **Paid-up:** regular premium only. After at least one full year's premium, an unpaid policy becomes paid-up. Paid-up death benefit during deferment is 105% of total premiums paid; after deferment it follows the option as described above. Paid-up annuity is the original annuity multiplied by premiums paid divided by premiums originally payable, subject to the minimum annuity; top-up annuity is unaffected.
- **Revival:** within 5 consecutive complete years from the first unpaid premium, on payment of arrears with interest and satisfactory continued insurability. The revival interest rate for 2026-27 is 9.50% p.a. compounding half-yearly.
- **Grace period:** 30 days for yearly and half-yearly modes; 15 days for quarterly and monthly modes.
- **Free look:** 30 days.

## Exclusions and conditions

- **Suicide:** if the annuitant commits suicide within 12 months from commencement or revival, the nominee receives the higher of 80% of total premiums paid (including top-up premium) or the surrender value, and the policy terminates. If the policy has lapsed without paid-up value, nothing is payable. After the deferment period, the death benefit under the chosen option applies.
- **Waiting period:** not applicable.
- **Annuity option:** chosen at inception and cannot be altered later.
- **Joint life:** restricted to the relationships listed in the brochure; the annuity is paid to the primary annuitant and then to the secondary annuitant at the same rate.
- **Minimum annuity:** if a paid-up annuity plus top-up falls below the minimum, and the policy is not revived within 5 years, the surrender value is paid and the policy terminates.
- **Taxes:** statutory taxes are charged over and above the premium and are not counted when calculating benefits.

## Tax treatment

The CIS states that taxes, if any, as applicable from time to time are charged extra. The brochure states that statutory taxes are payable over and above the premium, that tax paid is not considered when calculating benefits, and that income-tax benefits or implications should be discussed with a tax adviser. The brochure illustration notes that GST is currently exempted. The documents do not state the specific income-tax treatment of premiums, annuities or death benefits.

## Suitability and decision notes

**Best suited to:** pre-retirees aged 18 and above who want to build a guaranteed lifetime annuity with a deferment period of 1-15 years. It fits buyers who want a single-premium or regular-premium deferred annuity with a choice of 11 payout structures, couples and family members who want a joint-life annuity, and people who want return-of-premium protection through options 2-7, 9, 10 and 11.

**Poor fit for:** anyone who needs income immediately, investors who may need capital access, people seeking inflation-linked or market-linked returns, and buyers who may stop regular premiums early. It is also unsuitable for anyone unable to commit to the minimum premium, which rises sharply for short deferment periods.

**Key trade-offs:** no income during the deferment period; a fixed nominal annuity that is not indexed to inflation; formula-based surrender values that can return materially less than premiums paid; lower annuity rates under the return-of-premium options; an unpublished full rate table; and joint life restricted to defined family relationships.
